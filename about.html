About Us

The people and values behind AMFarmer Tax Services

Alida Miles Farmer

Tax Preparer / Owner

Alida Miles Farmer founded AMFarmer Tax Services with a simple mission: to provide every client with tax preparation that is quick, efficient, and completely confidential. With a commitment to accuracy and personalized service, she has built a practice that clients trust year after year.

Whether you're an individual filing a straightforward return or a business owner with more complex needs, AMFarmer Tax Services treats every client's situation with the same level of care and attention to detail.

We believe that tax preparation shouldn't be stressful. That's why we take the time to explain your return, answer your questions, and make sure you feel confident before we file. Our clients are more than just numbers — they're people we're committed to helping.

Serving clients both locally in the Burlington, NC area and nationwide through virtual consultations, AMFarmer Tax Services makes professional tax help accessible no matter where you are.
